Data deletion

Delete selected CryptKeep vault data without deleting your account.

CryptKeep lets you delete individual vault entries and categories while keeping your account active. Vault data is encrypted on your device before sync, so CryptKeep cannot read the plaintext contents of deleted or active vault records.

Entries can be deleted individually or in bulk.

Categories can be deleted separately from entries.

Your account remains active unless you request account deletion.

Delete vault entries in the app

  1. Open CryptKeep and sign in.
  2. Open your vault.
  3. To delete one entry, open the entry menu and choose Delete.
  4. To delete multiple entries, long-press an entry to enter selection mode, select the entries, then use the delete icon.
Deleted vault entries cannot be restored by CryptKeep.

Delete categories in the app

  1. Open CryptKeep and sign in.
  2. Open the vault menu and choose Manage Categories.
  3. Select the delete icon next to the category you want to remove.

Deleting a category does not delete the entries in that category. The entries remain in your vault and become uncategorized.

Request data deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send a data deletion request to eeriegoesd@gmail.com from the email address connected to your CryptKeep account. Include CryptKeep data deletion in the subject or message.

Because vault records are zero-knowledge encrypted, CryptKeep cannot inspect readable entry names, usernames, passwords, notes, or categories on your behalf. For specific item deletion, the in-app controls are the most precise method. By email, you can request deletion of broad data groups such as all encrypted vault entries, all encrypted categories, or your full account.

What can be deleted without deleting the account

Encrypted vault entries

Saved passwords, usernames, URLs, notes, and related entry fields are stored as encrypted vault records and can be deleted from the vault.

Encrypted categories

Category names are encrypted before storage and can be deleted separately from the entries assigned to them.

Partial data deletion does not delete your CryptKeep account, sign-in identity, or subscription status. To delete the account and associated vault data, use the account deletion page.

Zero-knowledge security model

CryptKeep is designed so the server does not receive your readable vault contents or your master password.

  • Your master password is used locally on your device.
  • CryptKeep derives a separate authentication password for sign-in, so the master password itself is not transmitted.
  • Your vault encryption key is derived locally with PBKDF2-SHA256 and a cryptographically random salt.
  • Vault entries are encrypted before storage using AES-256-GCM with a random nonce.
  • Category names are encrypted before they are stored.
CryptKeep cannot decrypt or read your saved passwords, notes, usernames, categories, or other vault fields.

Records outside the encrypted vault

Partial data deletion is for encrypted vault entries and categories. Some non-vault operational records can exist outside the encrypted vault, such as support emails you send, authentication/security logs, and subscription or payment records handled by an app store or payment provider.

These records do not contain decrypted vault entries. They may be kept only when needed for account security, abuse prevention, payment history, chargebacks, tax, legal, or compliance reasons.
