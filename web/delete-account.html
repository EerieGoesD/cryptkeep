Account deletion

Delete your CryptKeep account and encrypted vault data.

CryptKeep is built as a zero-knowledge password manager. Your vault entries are encrypted on your device before they are stored or synced. CryptKeep cannot read, recover, inspect, or export the plaintext contents of your vault.

Vault entries are encrypted client-side before sync.

The master password is not sent to CryptKeep.

Account deletion removes the account and encrypted vault records.

Delete your account in CryptKeep

  1. Open CryptKeep and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete account in the Danger Zone section.
  4. Confirm the deletion with your master password.
This action is permanent. After deletion, your CryptKeep account and encrypted vault records cannot be restored by CryptKeep.

If you cannot access the app

Send an account deletion request to eeriegoesd@gmail.com from the email address connected to your CryptKeep account. Include CryptKeep account deletion in the subject or message.

Email deletion requests are used as a backup path for users who cannot open the app or reach the in-app deletion control.

What CryptKeep deletes

Account data

Your CryptKeep account is deleted, including the account identity used to sign in to the app.

Encrypted vault records

Saved vault entries and categories stored by CryptKeep are deleted from the server. These records are stored as encrypted data, not readable plaintext.

Zero-knowledge security model

CryptKeep is designed so the server does not receive your readable vault contents or your master password.

  • Your master password is used locally on your device.
  • CryptKeep derives a separate authentication password for sign-in, so the master password itself is not transmitted.
  • Your vault encryption key is derived locally with PBKDF2-SHA256 and a cryptographically random salt.
  • Vault entries are encrypted before storage using AES-256-GCM with a random nonce.
  • Category names are also encrypted before they are stored.
Because CryptKeep does not have your master password or vault encryption key, it cannot decrypt your saved passwords, notes, usernames, categories, or other vault fields.

Records outside the encrypted vault

Account deletion is for your CryptKeep account and encrypted vault data. Some non-vault operational records can exist outside the encrypted vault, such as support emails you send, authentication/security logs, and subscription or payment records handled by an app store or payment provider.

These records do not contain decrypted vault entries. They may be kept only when needed for account security, abuse prevention, payment history, chargebacks, tax, legal, or compliance reasons.
