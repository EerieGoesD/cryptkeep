CryptKeep Account Deletion

This page explains how CryptKeep users can request deletion of their account and associated app data.

Delete your account in the app

Open CryptKeep, sign in, go to Settings, then choose Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm with your master password. This permanently deletes your CryptKeep account and vault data from the server.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email eeriegoesd@gmail.com from the email address connected to your CryptKeep account and request account deletion. Include the app name, CryptKeep, in the message.

What is deleted

Account deletion removes your CryptKeep account and associated encrypted vault data, including saved vault entries and categories stored by CryptKeep.

What may be kept

Some records may be retained only when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, payment, tax, dispute, or compliance purposes. Retained records are kept only for the required retention period.
